# Sample onboarding script (first dealer call)

**Duration:** 15–20 minutes  
**Audience:** Pilot dealer principal or sales manager

---

**Opening**

> Namaste — I'm [name] from EVSavari. We're in operational beta: we're sending **high-intent EV buyers** in [metro] to a small group of verified dealers. You were selected for [model 1], [model 2], and optionally [model 3].

**What EVSavari is**

> We're not a classifieds site. Buyers come through **decision guides** — city picks, comparisons, ownership topics — and when they're ready, they request a callback, book a test drive, or message us on WhatsApp. We route those enquiries to you with **vehicle and page context** already in the message.

**What we need from you today**

1. Complete your **dealer profile** (showroom name, city, phone)  
2. Publish **at least one accurate listing** (price, range, photo)  
3. Agree to respond to new leads within **4 business hours** on working days  

**Dashboard walkthrough**

> You'll use evsavari.com/dealer — Leads tab is your inbox. When you tap WhatsApp on a lead, the buyer's model and our page link are pre-filled so you don't start cold.

**Pilot boundaries**

> For this phase we're limiting to **[metro]** and **three models** so we can guarantee lead quality. We'll review together after two weeks — volume, response time, and any listing corrections.

**Questions to ask them**

- Which variants do you actually have in stock this month?  
- Who handles digital leads — same person as walk-ins?  
- Preferred language for buyer follow-up (English / Hindi / local)?

**Close**

> I'll send login details after this call. Please log in today and confirm you see a test lead. Our ops number for listing help is [ops WhatsApp]. Thank you — looking forward to the pilot.

---

*Internal only. Do not share lead scores, tiers, or behavioral exports.*
