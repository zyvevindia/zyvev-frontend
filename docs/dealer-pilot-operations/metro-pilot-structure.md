# 1-Metro Pilot Structure

## Objective

Validate that EVSavari can deliver **useful, privacy-safe buyer context** to a dealer partner without exposing internal scoring or raw behavioral data.

## Metro selection criteria

| Criterion | Guidance |
|-----------|----------|
| EV density | Strong EV consideration (NCR, Bangalore, Pune) |
| Charging infra | Mix of apartment + villa buyers |
| Dealer access | Warm intro to 1 partner (not cold mass outreach) |
| Ops capacity | Team can handle 5–15 leads/week manually |

## Recommended pilot metros

1. **Delhi NCR** — broad EV consideration, highway + apartment mix  
2. **Bangalore** — tech-forward buyers, apartment charging friction common  

Pick **one** for Week 2+ after live ops stable.

## Timeline

| Phase | Duration | Activity |
|-------|----------|----------|
| Prep | 3–5 days | Onboarding checklist, sample summaries |
| Live pilot | 14 days | Handoff leads with dealer-safe summaries |
| Review | 2 days | Feedback to `ops/dealer-feedback.jsonl` |

## Success signals (qualitative)

- Dealer finds summaries actionable for first call  
- No request for “lead scores” or raw analytics  
- Buyer range/charging expectations better set pre-visit  

## Out of scope

- CRM integration  
- Dealer login  
- Multi-city rollout  
- Public dealer rankings
