# High-Intent EV Buyer Indicators

**Internal use only.** Translate qualitatively in dealer summaries — never export raw events.

## Strong intent signals

| Signal | Dealer-safe phrasing |
|--------|----------------------|
| Compare completed (2+ models) | “Researched X vs Y on EVSavari before inquiring” |
| Ownership panel viewed | “Reviewed ownership guidance before reaching out” |
| Charging reality expanded | “Explored charging practicality for their situation” |
| SEO guide → detail → lead | “Arrived from [guide topic] — likely researching [use case]” |
| Bookmark saved | “Returned to vehicle after initial research” |

## Charging & apartment concern

| Signal | Dealer-safe phrasing |
|--------|----------------------|
| `charging_access` concern tag | “Asked about home/society charging setup” |
| `apartment` in concerns | “May need help with society AC / parking permission” |
| Multiple charging panel views | “Charging setup is a decision factor” |

## Ownership interest

| Signal | Dealer-safe phrasing |
|--------|----------------------|
| `ownership_uncertainty` concern | “Wants clarity on running costs and ownership comfort” |
| Service / warranty panel focus | “Ownership reassurance matters in their decision” |

## Compare engagement (without numbers)

- “Spent time comparing segment alternatives”  
- “Narrowed to two models in same price band”  

## What not to say

- ❌ “Lead score 82”  
- ❌ “Session ID …”  
- ❌ “Viewed page 14 times”  
- ❌ “Low quality lead”  

## Example dealer-safe paragraph

> Buyer inquired on Nexon EV Creative+ after comparing with Punch EV on EVSavari. They reviewed charging and ownership guidance, suggesting home charging setup is part of their decision. Recommend confirming society parking and AC access on first call.
