# Dealer Value Narrative

Use this language in dealer conversations. **Do not** mention internal scores, event counts, or “AI ranking.”

---

## What EVSavari is

EVSavari helps Indian buyers understand **which EV fits their real life** — charging, range planning, ownership comfort — before they talk to a dealer.

We are an **EV intelligence platform**, not a lead farm.

## What dealers get in the pilot

1. **Context-rich handoffs** — buyer compared specific models, viewed ownership/charging guidance  
2. **Better first-call quality** — fewer surprises on range and apartment charging  
3. **Brand-safe representation** — no public negative reviews; editorial governance on all copy  
4. **Feedback loop** — qualitative pilot learnings improve summaries over time  

## What dealers do not get (yet)

- Login dashboard or real-time analytics  
- Lead scores or “hotness” numbers  
- Guaranteed volume commitments  
- OEM-exclusive routing  

## Why now

- Tier-1 catalog with trust layer live  
- Controlled public beta — organic traffic only  
- Observation-backed guidance (internal moderation, not crowdsourced reviews)  

## Honest limitations

- Early traffic volume — pilot is learning, not scale  
- Not all variants have equal field observation depth  
- EVSavari does not handle financing, delivery, or service SLAs  

## Ask

> “Can we run a 2-week pilot on three models in [metro], with dealer-safe summaries on each lead, and a short feedback call mid-way?”
