# Pilot Scope

| Dimension | Value |
|-----------|--------|
| Duration | 2 weeks after stable Week 1 traffic |
| Metro | Delhi NCR **or** Bangalore (pick one) |
| Models | Nexon Creative+, Punch LR, Comet Play |
| Volume cap | ≤20 dealer conversations |
| Feedback | `ops/dealer-feedback.jsonl` |

## Success criteria

- Dealers find summaries **actionable** for first call
- No requests for raw analytics or public scores
- At least 3 documented follow-up outcomes

## Stop conditions

- PII leakage concern
- Misinterpretation of editorial guidance as guarantees
- Lead volume too low to learn (<5/week in metro)
