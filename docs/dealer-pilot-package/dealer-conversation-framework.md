# Dealer Conversation Framework

Structured flow for **first pilot conversation** (~30 minutes).

---

## 1. Opening (5 min)

**Goal:** Set expectations — intelligence partner, not CRM vendor.

- EVSavari helps buyers decide; you close and serve  
- Pilot: 1 metro, 3 models, 2 weeks  
- We share **qualitative context**, not scores or analytics dashboards  

## 2. Value story (8 min)

Use [trust-first-marketplace-narrative.md](./trust-first-marketplace-narrative.md) + [evsavari-positioning-summary.md](./evsavari-positioning-summary.md).

Highlight:

- Compare-assisted buyers  
- Trust/ownership engagement  
- Charging-concern clarity  

## 3. Example walkthrough (10 min)

Pick **one** example relevant to their inventory:

| Buyer type | Doc |
|------------|-----|
| Compared two SUVs | [compare-engaged-example.md](./compare-engaged-example.md) |
| Read trust/ownership | [trust-engaged-example.md](./trust-engaged-example.md) |
| Charging anxious | [charging-concern-example.md](./charging-concern-example.md) |
| Ownership focused | [ownership-intent-example.md](./ownership-intent-example.md) |

## 4. Operational alignment (5 min)

- Handoff: email with dealer-safe summary  
- Volume expectation: 5–15 leads / 2 weeks (organic)  
- Mid-pilot check-in: day 7  

## 5. Close (2 min)

- Confirm 3 pilot models + metro  
- Schedule pilot start (after Week 1 ops green)  
- Agree feedback call date  

---

## Objection handling

| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| “Send me lead scores” | We don’t score leads publicly — we send context narratives. |
| “How many leads guaranteed?” | Pilot is learning phase — organic volume only. |
| “Is this exclusive?” | No exclusivity in pilot. |
| “Do buyers see our inventory?” | EVSavari shows editorial catalog — leads route to you for fulfillment. |

---

## After the call

Log outcome in `ops/dealer-feedback.jsonl` and complete [dealer-onboarding-checklist.md](./dealer-onboarding-checklist.md).
