# EVSavari Positioning Summary

**For dealer conversations only — not a public marketing page.**

---

## One line

EVSavari helps Indian buyers choose the right EV for their **real life** — charging, range planning, and ownership comfort — before they speak to a dealer.

## What we are

- An **EV intelligence platform** for consideration and comparison  
- Editorial, governance-backed trust content (not crowdsourced reviews)  
- A source of **context-rich leads**, not anonymous form spam  

## What we are not

- A lead auction or aggregator  
- A dealer rating site  
- An OEM-configurator replacement  
- A financing or logistics platform  

## Buyer problem we solve

Buyers arrive with **charging anxiety**, **range confusion**, and **compare paralysis**. EVSavari structures decisions so your first call is more productive.

## Dealer benefit (pilot)

- Buyers who already compared specific models  
- Qualitative context on charging and ownership interests  
- No public negative reviews on your brand  

## Pilot ask

2-week pilot · 1 metro · 3 models · dealer-safe email summaries only.
