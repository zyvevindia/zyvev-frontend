# Trust-First Marketplace Narrative

Use this language when explaining EVSavari’s approach to dealers.

---

## Philosophy

Indian EV buyers do not need more hype — they need **honest planning tools**:

- How far will it really go for my commute?  
- Can I charge at home or in my society?  
- Is this EV comfortable for my family’s highway trips?  

EVSavari answers these with **editorial intelligence**, not star ratings or user reviews.

---

## Trust layer (what buyers see)

- **Range reality** — planning bands, not ARAI alone  
- **Charging practicality** — AC rhythm, DC expectations, stress level  
- **Ownership confidence** — service, warranty, running-cost framing  
- **Compare trust** — lifestyle-fit differences between models  

All content is **governed and moderated** — we do not publish open community reviews.

---

## Why dealers should care

- Buyers who use trust tools arrive **better informed** — fewer unrealistic range expectations  
- Compare-assisted buyers have **narrowed choice** — shorter sales cycles  
- You are not competing with a public scoreboard on EVSavari  

---

## What we will never do in pilot

- Publish dealer rankings  
- Show lead scores to dealers  
- Expose raw clickstream or session data  

---

## Proof points (internal)

- 29 Tier-1 variants with structured trust blocks  
- 100+ governed editorial observations (internal moderation)  
- Privacy-first anonymous behavioral learning (no PII in events)
