# Trusted authority expansion roadmap

Practical EV intelligence clusters — editorial depth only.

## Clusters (aligned with `seoAuthorityOps`)

1. **EV ownership reality** — running cost, insurance, TCO guides
2. **Apartment charging practicality** — society/RWA, workplace top-ups
3. **City vs highway usage** — discover hubs + highway ownership guide
4. **Running-cost explainers** — `/guides/ownership-running-cost`
5. **EV beginner guidance** — under-15-lakh / first-time framing
6. **Family EV practicality** — family-friendly discovery
7. **Charging behavior expectations** — home charging guide, DC planning
8. **Long-distance suitability** — highway ownership + long-trip planning

## Linking discipline

- Compare pages: `buildCompareAuthorityLinks()` (max 4 contextual links)
- Detail pages: `buildVehicleDiscoveryLinkSections()`
- SEO report: `compareToGuideLinks` for high-traffic pairs

## Metrics (existing ops)

- `authorityDepthTrend` — topicalAuthorityScore bands
- `weakAuthorityClusters` — cluster completeness < 55
- `underlinkedComparePages` — traffic without manifest compare
- `trustContentOpportunities` — high guideOpportunityScore
- `guideUsefulnessSignals` — usefulness_feedback buffer rate

## Cadence

- **Bi-weekly:** deepen one cluster (editorial review, not bulk pages)
- **Weekly:** export guide opportunities from `/admin/seo-authority`
- **Before scaling traffic:** verify internal links on top 5 compare pairs

## Out of scope

- AI article farms, mass city×model pages, keyword stuffing
