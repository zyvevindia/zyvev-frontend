## Operations cadence

- **Weekly** — freshness check on top practical hubs; review ops panels for weak freshness.  
- **On catalog change** — update running-cost and range references before promoting compare pairs.  
- **On trust signal** — pair-level editorial review when distrust recurs under usage.  

## Principles

- Usefulness and realism first; calm tone always.  
- No AI article farms, thin SEO, or spam scaling.  
- Compare links only where they reduce buyer doubt.
