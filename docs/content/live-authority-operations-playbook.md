# Live authority operations playbook

**Purpose:** Keep EVSavari’s practical ownership content fresh and trustworthy under real traffic — without thin SEO, AI farms, or spam scaling.

## Editorial scope

1. Apartment charging  
2. EV ownership practicality  
3. Running-cost reality  
4. Beginner EV guidance  
5. Charging expectations  
6. Family EV practicality  
7. Long-distance EV suitability  
8. EV buying decisions  
9. Charging myths  
10. Ownership decision support  

## Live operations principles

- **Freshness** — revisit hubs when catalog, incentives, or charging norms shift.  
- **Usefulness first** — one clear answer per hub; no duplicate TCO pages.  
- **Realism first** — align with ownership intelligence and trust calibration.  
- **Calm tone** — no urgency, no acquisition tricks.  

## Ops surfaces

- `/admin/content-usefulness` — freshest authority, durable explainers, weak freshness  
- `/admin/public-beta-ops` — live platform + real traffic readiness  
- Weekly snapshot: `evsavari-live-platform-weekly-v1`
