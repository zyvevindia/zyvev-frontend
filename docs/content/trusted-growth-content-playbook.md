# Trusted growth content playbook

EVSavari grows as a **trusted public EV ownership authority** through practical content — not thin SEO or AI article farms.

## Priority topics

1. Apartment charging reality  
2. EV ownership practicality  
3. Running-cost expectations  
4. Beginner EV guidance  
5. Charging behavior reality  
6. Family EV practicality  
7. Long-distance EV usage  
8. EV buying decision guidance  
9. EV charging myths  
10. Ownership decision support  

## Ops review (`/admin/content-usefulness`)

| Panel | Action |
|-------|--------|
| Highest trust-retention authority content | Deepen editorial; keep specs current |
| Most durable ownership guides | Link from high-traffic compares |
| Most useful charging explainers | Align with charging practicality ops |
| Weak practical authority clusters | Fix or merge — no new thin URLs |
| Strong compare-support authority | Ensure compare pages link both ownership + charging |

## Persistence signals

- `authorityUsefulnessPersistence`  
- `practicalGuideTrustRetention`  
- `ownershipGuideDurability`  
- `chargingGuideUsefulnessPersistence`  
- `practicalContentRevisitQuality`  

## Rules

- Realism-first caveats  
- Usefulness-first internal links  
- No spam scaling or geo-farm pages  
- Human-reviewed updates only  

## Scaling gate

Expand content marketing only when `practicalGuideTrustRetention` is `trusted` and weak clusters are shrinking week-over-week.
