# Trusted authority retention playbook

Practical EV ownership authority on EVSavari is **realism-first** and **usefulness-first**. This playbook ties editorial focus to retention signals — not thin-content scaling or AI article farms.

## Priority practical clusters

1. Apartment & society charging  
2. EV ownership practicality & running cost  
3. Beginner EV guidance  
4. Charging expectations & myths  
5. Family & long-distance suitability  
6. Ownership decision support  

## Retention signals (content ops)

Review on `/admin/content-usefulness`:

| Panel | Action |
|-------|--------|
| Highest trust-retention content | Double down on editorial depth and internal links |
| Most durable ownership guides | Keep specs and caveats current |
| Weak practical authority clusters | Editorial pass or merge — do not spawn new URLs |
| Most revisited charging guides | Add compare-support links from top pairs |
| Strong compare-support authority | Ensure compare pages link to these guides |

## Editorial rules

- **One honest caveat per section** — range, charging, and cost bands are directional.
- **No spam SEO** — no mass city variants without unique practical value.
- **No AI article farms** — human-reviewed updates only.
- **Compare support** — every high-traffic compare should link to at least one ownership and one charging guide.

## Persistence metrics

- `authorityTrustPersistence` — repeat guide paths across sessions  
- `guideReturnQuality` — revisit depth on ownership/charging hubs  
- `chargingGuideUsefulnessPersistence` — charging cluster engagement trend  
- `weakAuthorityContentPersistence` — clusters that stay weak after calibration  

## Scaling gate

Expand authority publishing only when:

- `practicalContentRetentionQuality` is `trusted` or improving  
- `weakPracticalAuthorityClusters` is shrinking week-over-week  
- `compareAssistedGuideUsefulness` is `strong` or `emerging` with buffer evidence  

## Rollback

If guide traffic rises but `contentTrustTrend` falls, pause new hub pages and fix misleading sections before adding URLs.
