# Public authority operations playbook

EVSavari’s public authority is built on **practical usefulness** and **ownership realism** — not volume SEO or AI-generated articles.

## Editorial focus

| Cluster | Public role |
|---------|-------------|
| Apartment & society charging | Trust entry for urban buyers |
| Ownership running cost | TCO realism |
| Charging expectations | Myths vs practice |
| Family & highway suitability | Decision support |
| Beginner guidance | First-time EV transition |

## Ops review (`/admin/content-usefulness`)

| Panel | Action |
|-------|--------|
| Most trusted public authority content | Maintain accuracy; link from top compares |
| Highest authority retention guides | Refresh when specs or policy change |
| Weak public authority clusters | Fix or consolidate — no new thin URLs |
| Best ownership explainers | Align with ownership realism ops |
| Best charging practicality content | Align with charging practicality ops |

## Signals

- `authorityVisibilityTrend` — public engagement trajectory  
- `trustedAuthorityEntryQuality` — guide-first session quality  
- `authorityRevisitPersistence` — repeat guide paths  
- `compareAfterGuideTrust` — guide → compare completion  
- `authorityAssistedCompareDepth` — depth after authority entry  

## Rules

- Realism-first caveats on every hub  
- Usefulness-first internal linking  
- No spam SEO or mass geo pages  
- No AI article farms  

## Scaling gate

Expand public visibility only when `authorityVisibilityTrend` is `visible` and `weakPublicAuthorityClusters` is shrinking.
