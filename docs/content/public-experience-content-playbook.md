# Public experience content playbook

**Purpose:** Polish EVSavari’s public authority content around practical ownership — without thin SEO, AI article farms, or spam scaling.

## Editorial scope (only these topics)

1. Apartment charging  
2. EV ownership practicality  
3. Running-cost reality  
4. Beginner EV guidance  
5. Charging expectations  
6. Family EV practicality  
7. Long-distance EV suitability  
8. EV buying decisions  
9. Charging myths  
10. Ownership decision support  

## Principles

- **Usefulness first** — each guide answers one real buyer question.  
- **Realism first** — align with ownership intelligence and trust calibration.  
- **Compare support** — link from compare only when it reduces doubt.  
- **Calm tone** — no urgency, no sales pressure.  

## What to avoid

- Mass-generated locality pages without review  
- Duplicate hubs with the same TCO paragraph  
- Internal links that bypass explainable compare scoring  

## Ops surfaces

- `/admin/content-usefulness` — polished journeys, clarity, authority guides  
- `/admin/seo-authority` — discovery consistency, compare-support authority  
- Weekly snapshot: `evsavari-public-experience-weekly-v1`
