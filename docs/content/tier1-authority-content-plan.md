# Tier-1 authority content plan

**Phase:** Day 3 — Real content foundation  
**Tone:** Practical, calm, trustworthy, beginner-friendly — no hype, no AI spam  
**Goal:** EV ownership authority that supports compare and catalog operations

## Principles

1. **Useful before rankable** — answer real buyer questions with honest trade-offs.
2. **Beginner-first** — assume first-time EV buyer in an Indian city.
3. **Evidence-backed** — cite charging behavior, running costs, and ownership friction honestly.
4. **Calm UX alignment** — short sections, plain language, no urgency marketing.
5. **Tier-1 catalog tie-in** — link to persistent families and compare routes where relevant.

## Content pillars (8 topics)

### 1. Apartment charging

**Working title:** EV charging in apartments and RWAs — what actually works in India  
**Audience:** Urban buyers in gated societies  
**Key questions**

- Can you charge without a dedicated parking spot?
- RWA approval patterns and common objections
- AC vs portable charger practicality
- Which tier-1 EVs suit slow overnight AC (Comet, Tiago, Punch vs Nexon)

**Tier-1 links:** `tata-tiago-ev`, `mg-comet-ev`, `tata-nexon-ev`, ownership-society-rwa SEO  
**Trust angle:** Set realistic expectations; avoid "every apartment is easy"

---

### 2. Real EV running cost

**Working title:** What an EV really costs to run in India (not brochure math)  
**Audience:** Cost-conscious first-time buyers  
**Key questions**

- Electricity vs petrol per km (city driving)
- Service intervals and typical maintenance
- Insurance and tyre wear
- When an EV pays back vs a comparable ICE

**Tier-1 links:** Compare pairs by budget (Tiago vs Comet, Punch vs Nexon)  
**Trust angle:** Show ranges and assumptions; link to TCO ownership pages

---

### 3. Beginner EV ownership

**Working title:** Your first EV in India — a calm starter guide  
**Audience:** Never owned an EV  
**Key questions**

- Daily range anxiety — what's normal
- Home vs public charging rhythm
- Apps and payment friction
- First-week checklist

**Tier-1 links:** `tata-tiago-ev`, `mg-comet-ev`, beginner-friendly ownership flags  
**Trust angle:** Normalize learning curve; no "EVs are effortless everywhere"

---

### 4. EV charging myths

**Working title:** EV charging myths Indians still believe (and what's true)  
**Audience:** Research-phase browsers from ICE background  
**Key questions**

- "EVs can't charge in rain"
- "Fast charging ruins batteries overnight"
- "You need DC every day"
- Grid / load-shedding concerns

**Tier-1 links:** Charging-port media roles; city charging guides  
**Trust angle:** Debunk with nuance, not dismissal

---

### 5. City EV practicality

**Working title:** Which EVs make sense for daily city driving in India  
**Audience:** Commuters in metro / tier-2 cities  
**Key questions**

- Stop-go efficiency
- Parking and turning radius
- Public charger density by city pattern
- Heat and AC impact on range

**Tier-1 links:** City SEO pages; Punch, Comet, Tiago, Nexon  
**Trust angle:** City-specific honesty (Delhi vs Kochi vs Pune)

---

### 6. Family EV guidance

**Working title:** Choosing a family EV in India — space, safety, and charging  
**Audience:** 4–5 seat household buyers  
**Key questions**

- Boot space and child-seat fit
- Rear seat comfort and AC performance
- Highway suitability for weekend trips
- Service network proximity

**Tier-1 links:** `tata-nexon-ev`, `mg-zs-ev`, `mahindra-xev-9e`, `byd-atto-3`  
**Trust angle:** Compare trade-offs; avoid "best family EV" absolutes

---

### 7. Long-distance EV practicality

**Working title:** Highway EV trips in India — planning without stress  
**Audience:** Buyers who drive inter-city occasionally  
**Key questions**

- Real highway range vs claimed
- DC stop planning on NH corridors
- Time cost vs ICE
- Which tier-1 EVs are highway-suitable

**Tier-1 links:** Nexon vs Curvv, BE 6 vs XEV 9e compares; highway ownership SEO  
**Trust angle:** Honest about gaps on certain routes

---

### 8. First-time EV buying

**Working title:** How to buy your first EV in India — step-by-step  
**Audience:** Ready to shortlist / test drive  
**Key questions**

- Test drive checklist (charging, regen, ground clearance)
- Variant selection without overspending
- Dealer questions about warranty and home install
- When to use compare vs detail pages

**Tier-1 links:** Full tier-1 catalog; lead funnel; compare pairs  
**Trust angle:** Empower decision-making; no dealer pressure language

## Production workflow (human-governed)

1. **Outline** — one page per pillar; H2 = buyer question
2. **Draft** — human writer; max ~1,200 words per pillar
3. **Catalog QA** — verify linked slugs exist in tier-1 definitions + sitemap
4. **Trust review** — ops/trust checklist; no unverifiable claims
5. **SEO pass** — title, description, internal links; no thin duplication
6. **Publish** — static SEO JSON or CMS path per existing content pipeline

## Quality bar (reject if)

- Generic AI filler without India-specific context
- Unverified range or price claims
- "Best EV" without audience qualifier
- Missing link to at least one tier-1 family or compare route
- Hype language ("revolutionary", "must buy now")
