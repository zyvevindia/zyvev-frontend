# Trusted scaling content playbook

**Purpose:** Grow EVSavari authority and practical guides without thin SEO, AI article farms, or spam scaling.

## Principles

1. **Usefulness first** — every guide must answer a real ownership or charging question.
2. **Realism first** — range, charging, and cost claims stay calibrated to EVSavari ownership intelligence.
3. **Compare support** — authority pages link into compare only where they reduce doubt, not to inflate URLs.
4. **Memorability** — repeat visits to the same practical guide signal value; do not chase one-off clicks.

## What to expand

| Cluster | Examples | Link from |
|---------|----------|-----------|
| Ownership reality | Running cost, warranty, resale | Compare, car detail |
| Charging practicality | Home, society, highway | Compare, discover |
| City / highway suitability | Discovery hubs | Compare after guide read |

## What to avoid

- Mass-generated city or comparison pages without editorial review
- Duplicate hubs that restate the same TCO paragraph
- Internal links that bypass the compare engine or trust calibration

- `/admin/seo-authority` — memorable authority, weak retention paths, underlinked guides
