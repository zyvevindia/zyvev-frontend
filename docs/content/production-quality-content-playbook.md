# Production-quality content playbook

**Purpose:** Ship practical EV ownership content at production quality — no thin SEO, AI farms, or spam scaling.

## Editorial scope

1. Apartment charging  
2. EV ownership practicality  
3. Running-cost reality  
4. Beginner EV guidance  
5. Charging expectations  
6. Family EV practicality  
7. Long-distance EV suitability  
8. EV buying decisions  
9. Charging myths  
10. Ownership decision support  

## Quality bar

- **Usefulness first** — one clear question answered per hub.  
- **Realism first** — aligned with ownership intelligence and trust calibration.  
- **Production polish** — readable headings, calm tone, working internal links.  
- **Compare support** — link from high-traffic compare only when it reduces doubt.  

## Ops surfaces

- `/admin/content-usefulness` — highest-quality journeys, authority content, weak practical quality  
- `/admin/seo-authority` — discovery and compare-support authority  
- Weekly snapshot: `evsavari-production-launch-weekly-v1`
